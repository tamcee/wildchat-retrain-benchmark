Strategy Architecture & Empirical Findings

A structural comparison of why specific continual learning mechanisms succeed or fail under real-world prompt distribution drift.

Scheduled Retraining

Retrains model periodically on a fixed calendar schedule using the most recent 12-week sliding window.

  • every_week: Establishes the upper performance bound (0.4834 F1) but consumes 45 retraining runs (204.4s).
  • every_4_weeks (Pareto Winner): Recovers 82.3% of the weekly retraining lift (0.4764 F1) with 75.6% fewer retrains (11 vs 45) and identical low decay weeks (1.2 vs 1.0).

Drift-Triggered (No Labels)

Monitors input vocabulary shift and predicted label distribution in real time without ground-truth labels.

  • drift_2σ: Triggers 11.2 retrains, achieving 0.4725 F1 and only 1.2 degraded weeks.
  • drift_3σ: Triggers 9.2 retrains (0.4704 F1), offering balanced sensitivity.
  • Advantage: Preempts accuracy drops before users experience them, without requiring delayed manual annotation.

Score-Triggered (Needs Labels)

Fires when rolling 2-week macro F1 drops below reference threshold. Inherently reactive.

  • score_0.02: 3.8 retrains, 0.4639 F1, 2.0 degraded weeks.
  • score_0.03: 2.6 retrains, 0.4597 F1, 2.8 degraded weeks.
  • score_0.05: 1.2 retrains, 0.4541 F1, 4.0 degraded weeks.
  • Limitation: Reactive lag forces users to experience a degradation period before the system responds.
